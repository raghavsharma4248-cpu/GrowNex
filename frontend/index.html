UN Sustainable Development Goal 8

Decent work and economic growth starts with being found.

Small and micro businesses employ a large share of working people, yet many have no online presence at all. GrowNex is a small step towards closing that gap.

  • A storefront without the costOwners list products and prices without paying for a website, a developer or a commission on sales.
  • Direct contact, no middlemanEnquiries go straight to the business, which replies to the customer by email. The sale stays with the seller.
  • Room for first-time foundersHome kitchens, weavers and repair shops get the same profile page as an established store.
